# V16Q — Controlled Deeper Research Queue

## Objective
Turn V16P's current-event, multi-evidence research population into the first controlled queue for additional machine research.

## Admission
A record must have current ownership evidence, assessment context, property context, and at least one validated recent combination reason from V16N/V16O:
- multiple recorded events in the last 365 days;
- recent conveyance with prior estate/fiduciary record; or
- recent conveyance with prior court/foreclosure/tax record.

A recent estate/fiduciary record by itself is not an admission rule. Multiple conveyances over three years by itself is not an admission rule. Price, value, acreage, prestige and luxury status are not admission rules.

## Meaning
`DEEPER_RESEARCH` authorizes more machine research only. It does not mean seller intent, seller motivation, distress, lead status, INVESTIGATE, contact authorization, or outreach.

## Safety / governance
Read only. No database writes. No owner names/addresses emitted. No seller scoring. No outreach. No operational state mutation.

## Endpoint
`/api/intelligence/deeper-research-queue-v16q`
