PMI FIND16 — Land-Led Replacement Economics Discovery Experiment

Canonical package: PMI_FIND16.zip
Endpoint: /api/intelligence/find16

Purpose
- Test OU-002 obsolete improvement on valuable land and OU-003 teardown/rebuild economics as a genuinely distinct combination mechanism.
- Existing evidence only: PROPERTY_CONTEXT + ASSESSMENT_CONTEXT + current discovered-universe comparison.
- No new data source and no generic enrichment.

Required mechanism
1. Land carries unusually high relative economic weight within compatible property-class peers.
2. Existing improvement is economically or physically light relative to compatible peers.
3. Same-street micro-corridor shows observed recent reinvestment/replacement activity.

Critical limits
- Assessment relationships are not market prices.
- Year built is not condition.
- Same-street context is not true adjacency.
- Reinvestment context is not subject buildability.
- Candidate does not prove teardown feasibility, rebuild economics, entitlement, demolition suitability, seller intent, or contact authority.
- Experimental WHB thresholds are observability thresholds only, not universal teardown rules.
- Route kill never equals property kill.

Pass condition
Promote only if the three-part mechanism adds meaningful novel discovery or materially distinct corroboration beyond the current land-utilization, age-mismatch, and redevelopment-halo rails. Otherwise kill the rail without adding data.
